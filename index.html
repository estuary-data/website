What We Do

Three focused services for nonprofits navigating platform changes and data challenges.

Data Migration

Move your contacts, donors, and history to a new platform cleanly and completely. We map your data, handle the transfer, and verify the results — so nothing gets lost in the move.

Platform Integration

Connect your fundraising, program, and communication tools so data flows automatically between them. No more manual exports, copy-paste, or duplicate entry.

Data Cleanup

Deduplicate, standardize, and validate your existing data before a migration or on its own. Start fresh with a database you can actually trust.

Built for nonprofits

We work with small and mid-size nonprofits — typically organizations with 20 to 200 staff and limited IT resources. Our clients are switching CRMs, connecting a new case management system, or cleaning up years of inconsistent data before an audit or board review.

We have particular experience with organizations in special education and human services — sectors where data accuracy isn't just an operational issue, it's a compliance and funding one. We understand the stakes and we know the platforms.

You don't need a technical background to work with us. We handle the complexity and keep you informed at every step.

How It Works

A clear, three-step process — designed for organizations that don't have time to manage a complicated project.

1

Scoping Call

We talk through your data, your platforms, and your timeline. You leave with a clear picture of what the work involves and what it will cost — no surprises.

2

Migration or Integration

We do the technical work. You review the output at key checkpoints and give feedback. Your team's time is protected — we're not handing you a to-do list.

3

Delivery
